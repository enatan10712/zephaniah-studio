# Images Directory Structure

## Logo
- `logo.png` - Your Zephaniah Studio logo (used throughout the site)

## Product Images
- `products/` - Product photography and lifestyle shots
  - Naming convention: `product-[id]-[index].jpg`
  - Examples: `product-1-1.jpg`, `product-1-2.jpg`, `product-2-1.jpg`
  - Recommended size: 1200x1200px for product shots
  - Format: JPG or PNG

## Showroom Images
- `showroom/` - Physical showroom photography
  - Interior shots of your showroom
  - Furniture displays and arrangements
  - Recommended size: 1920x1080px for hero images
  - Format: JPG for high-quality photos

## Gallery Images
- `gallery/` - Portfolio and showcase images
  - Completed projects
  - Customer installations
  - Craftsmanship details
  - Recommended size: 1200x800px
  - Format: JPG

## Usage in Components

### Product Cards
- Use product images from `public/images/products/`
- Multiple images per product for gallery views

### Hero Sections
- Use showroom images for background
- High-impact lifestyle photography

### Gallery Pages
- Showcase completed projects
- Display craftsmanship details

## Image Optimization Tips
1. Use WebP format for better performance
2. Compress images without losing quality
3. Maintain consistent aspect ratios
4. Use descriptive alt text for SEO
5. Implement lazy loading for better performance
